There are two glass things in Zhangjiajie

This page

Grand Canyon Glass Bridge

A free-standing suspension bridge spanning a canyon in Cili County, 430 m long, glass the entire way. About 30–40 minutes from Wulingyuan town, and roughly 60 km from Tianmen Mountain. Its own scenic area, its own ticket, its own day.

Not this page

Tianmen Mountain skywalks

Short glass panels bolted onto a cliff face high on Tianmen Mountain, above the city. Three separate sections, a few yuan each for shoe covers, and a stone-floor bypass beside every one.

The numbers

How high is it, really?

The span is uncontested: 430 metres long and six metres wide, opened in August 2016 and designed by the Israeli architect Haim Dotan, who wanted a bridge that would disappear into the gorge rather than dominate it.

The height is where sources diverge. You will see anything from 260 to 400 metres quoted for the same bridge, because the canyon floor is not flat and different operators measure from different points. The bungee platform at mid-span is generally given as about 260 metres above the ground beneath it; the deck is commonly described as roughly 300 metres above the valley floor.

None of this changes the experience. What you should take from it: any page quoting a single confident figure to the metre is repeating a number rather than measuring one.

On the day

The route through the canyon

The bridge is the entrance, not the destination. The walk down through the gorge is the longer and better half of the ticket.

  • Stop 01The bridge. You enter from the western tourist centre and cross first. The middle section is the fully transparent stretch where everyone lies down for photographs.
  • Stop 02Qiaodong experience zone. Where the bungee, zip line, sky slide and via ferrata launch from. Skip entirely if you only want the walk.
  • Stop 03The descent. Glass plank road, then a series of sightseeing elevators cut into the canyon wall, dropping you towards the floor.
  • Stop 04Shenquan Gorge and One-Line Sky. The narrowest part of the canyon, walls close enough to touch on both sides. The best photography of the day is here, not on the bridge.
  • Stop 05Shenquan Lake boat. A short cruise out to the Shuangping exit, where the shuttles wait. Allow for a queue in summer.
The short version If your schedule is tight, cross the bridge and turn back out through the side gate near the bridge rather than descending. That takes under an hour but forfeits the gorge and the boat — which is most of what you paid for.
Logistics

Getting there, and when to arrive

From Wulingyuan. Roughly 15 kilometres from Zhangjiajie National Forest Park, about thirty to forty minutes by road. Direct tourist buses run from Wulingyuan bus station; a taxi or chartered car is the low-friction option for a group.

From Zhangjiajie city or Tianmen Mountain. Around 60 kilometres, closer to an hour and a half with traffic. If you are basing yourself in the city, this is a committed half-day.

Opening hours. Typically from around 08:00 or 09:00, with last entry in the mid-afternoon. Hours shift seasonally, so check the day before.

Daily caps. Entry is capacity-limited and timed. In July, August and the October holiday the day's allocation can be gone before noon, so book online in advance rather than turning up.

Weather closures. The bridge shuts in high wind, and glass activities can close in ice. There is no way to predict this more than a day out; keep the canyon flexible in your plan rather than pinning a flight to it.

The jump

Bungee from the middle of the bridge

A platform at mid-span drops jumpers into the gorge from around 260 metres, which puts it among the highest commercial bungee operations anywhere. It is run by a specialist operator rather than the scenic area itself, and it is expensive — expect somewhere in the region of ¥2,000 on booking platforms, higher at the gate.

Eligibility is strict and enforced: a minimum age of 16, with exclusions for heart conditions, high blood pressure, pregnancy and various spinal conditions. Confirm your own eligibility before you travel rather than assuming staff will catch every contraindication at the platform.

For a fraction of the cost and most of the view, the zip line across the gorge does a lot of the same work.

Availability The bungee does not run every day. Weather, maintenance and demand all close it, and it is not included in any bridge ticket. Treat it as a bonus rather than the reason you came.
The order that works

Bridge first, then walk down

The crossing itself takes about fifteen minutes. Do it while you are fresh, then descend the canyon past the waterfalls and boat out across the lake. The walk below is the better half of the same ticket.

Questions

Glass bridge FAQ

Is the Zhangjiajie glass bridge safe?

The deck uses thick laminated safety glass, is inspected on a regular schedule, and the bridge closes pre-emptively in high wind. It has a strong operating record since 2016. The honest caveat is comfort rather than engineering: there is no non-glass route across, so if heights are a genuine problem for you, buy the canyon-only ticket and enjoy the gorge from below.

How long does it take to walk across?

Ten to fifteen minutes at a normal pace, longer if you stop for photographs in the transparent middle section. The full canyon route below the bridge takes two to four hours depending on which activities you add.

Can I do the glass bridge and Tianmen Mountain in one day?

Not comfortably. They sit around sixty kilometres apart, each needs a timed entry slot, and Tianmen Mountain alone runs four to six hours. Give them separate days, or drop one.

Do I need to book in advance?

Yes in summer and around Chinese public holidays, when the daily entry cap can sell out before lunchtime. On a spring or autumn weekday you can usually buy a slot a day or two ahead.

Are shoe covers required?

On the Grand Canyon bridge, covers are handed out at the entrance to protect the glass. On Tianmen Mountain's cliff skywalks they are a small separate charge per section. Either way you will not be walking on glass in your own shoes.

Is the canyon worth it if I skip the bridge?

Genuinely, yes. Shenquan Gorge, the waterfall path and the lake cruise stand on their own, and the canyon-only ticket costs a third of the combined one. It is the sensible choice for anyone travelling with someone who will not step onto glass.
